Company Overview

Founded in 2015, LogiXpress has grown to become a leading provider of warehouse and logistics management solutions. Our team of industry experts combines decades of experience in supply chain management with cutting-edge technology to deliver exceptional results for our clients.

We serve over 500 businesses across various industries, helping them optimize their operations, reduce costs, and improve customer satisfaction through our comprehensive platform.
