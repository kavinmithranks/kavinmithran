Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add new inventory items? +
Navigate to the Inventory page and click the "Add New Item" button. Fill in the product details including name, SKU, category, quantity, and price. You can also upload product images and set reorder thresholds.
How can I track shipment status? +
Go to the Shipments page and locate your shipment using the tracking number or order ID. Click the tracking icon next to the shipment to view real-time updates, estimated delivery, and carrier information.
How do I generate custom reports? +
Visit the Reports page and select "Custom Report Builder." Choose your report type, date range, and metrics. You can export reports as PDF, Excel, or CSV formats for further analysis.
Can I integrate LogiXpress with other systems? +
Yes! LogiXpress supports integration with popular e-commerce platforms, accounting software, and shipping carriers. Visit the Settings > Integrations page to configure your connections.
